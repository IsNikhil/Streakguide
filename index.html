How to Know If Your Email Was Opened (Streak for Gmail)

Nikhil Shah · ENGL3220 · Fall 2026

This guide shows you how to see when someone opens your Gmail message, like a read receipt. You will use a free Chrome extension called Streak. It takes about 10 minutes.

Before you start

You need four things:

  • @A Gmail account
  • Google Chrome on a laptop or desktop computer
  • About 10 minutes
  • 2A second email address to send a test email to (your school email works)
How it works: Streak adds a tiny, hidden picture to the email you send. When the reader opens the email, that picture loads. Streak sees this and shows you a "view."

Part 1 6 steps

Install Streak

1

Open the Streak page.

Open Chrome. Click this link: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/streak-crm-for-gmail/pnnfemgpilpdaojpnkjdgfgbnnjojfik. It opens the Streak page in the Chrome Web Store.

2

Add Streak to Chrome.

Click the blue "Add to Chrome" button.

3

Confirm.

A small box pops up near the top of Chrome. It asks if you want to add Streak. Click "Add extension".

4

Open Gmail.

Go to mail.google.com. If Gmail is already open, refresh the page.

5

Give Streak permission.

Streak asks for permission to work with your Gmail. Pick your Google account and click "Allow".

6

Choose email tracking.

A window opens that says "Where do you want to start?" Click "Email & link tracking". The box gets a blue border when you pick it. Then click "Next".

Part 2 4 steps

Turn on tracking and send a test email

1

Start a new email.

In Gmail, click "Compose" in the top left.

2

Write the email.

Type your second email address in the "To" line. Add a subject and a short message. In our test, the subject was "streak crm" and the message was "Find out if my email is opened?"

3

Check the eye icon.

Look at the bottom of the email window, next to the blue Send button. You will see a row of small icons. Find the eye. When the eye is orange, tracking is on. If it is not orange, click it once.

4

Click Send.

Click the blue "Send" button. Streak now watches this email.

Part 3 4 steps

See if your email was opened

1

Look at the Tracking panel.

Right after you send, a "Tracking" panel shows on the right side of Gmail. It says "No views" and "Sent seconds ago." This means nobody has opened the email yet.

2

Open the email as the reader.

Open your second email account and open the message you sent. Wait a few seconds. Then go back to the Tracking panel in your first account.

3

Read the new result.

The panel now says "Viewed seconds ago." It also shows the total views and total clicks. Each row below is one view. Each row shows a device icon, a place, and the time. In our test, there were 2 views at 3:11 PM, and the place was "Unknown location."

4

Check your Sent list.

You can also see the result without opening the email. Go to your Sent folder. A green eye shows next to the message once it is opened. The word next to the eye tells you how long ago it was opened. Here, it says "seconds."

What the results mean

A "view" means the hidden picture in your email loaded. It is a good sign that the email was opened, but it is not perfect. Keep these limits in mind:

The place can be unknown.

In our test, the place was "Unknown location." Email apps like Gmail often hide the reader's real place.

The number of views is not exact.

One person can make more than one view, for example by opening the email on a phone and a laptop.

No views does not mean unread.

If the reader blocks images, Streak cannot see the open.

The free plan shows limited details.

The panel says "Upgrade for more info" for extra features. You do not need to pay to follow this guide.

Use tracking in a fair way. It works well for school, club, and job emails, such as checking if a teacher or a team saw your message.

Quick fixes

No Tracking panel

Refresh Gmail. Then check that the eye icon was orange before you clicked Send.

Streak is missing in Gmail

Open chrome://extensions in a new Chrome tab. Make sure the switch next to Streak is on.

Still "No views"

Wait a minute and refresh. Also make sure you sent the test to a different email address, not the same account you sent it from.

Reader blocks images

Streak cannot count the view in this case. This is normal.
